# Docaya artwork generation prompts

Generated on **6 September 2026** with the **built-in image-generation tool**. These are the exact final prompts for the current open-page identity. The selected PNGs were copied into the repository without pixel edits. Both logos have genuine transparent alpha; the welcome illustration has an opaque ivory background.

See the [brand guide](branding.md) for downloads, previews and usage. Earlier unversioned assets remain in `public/brand/` as a previous exploration; the application uses the `-v2` assets below.

## Docaya logo

Saved as [public/brand/docaya-mark-v2.png](../public/brand/docaya-mark-v2.png). New image, no reference image.

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a completely fresh premium logo SYMBOL for Docaya, a contemporary UAE document-intelligence platform. This is an OPEN, AIRY, FLOWING-PAGES identity, not a monogram. No letter D, no box, no badge, no shield, no folder icon, no speech bubble, no sparkle. Design exactly three confident smooth ribbon/page shapes sweeping upward and outward from a shared lower centre, suggesting an open book becoming a light upward flight. The largest emerald-green page sweeps to the upper right; a smaller vivid-red page opens to the upper left; a slim charcoal-black lower page/arc supports the composition. Broad crisp white negative-space channels keep the three colours visibly separate. Overall silhouette is asymmetric but precisely balanced, elegant, optimistic, fluid and unmistakably modern. Think a world-class contemporary software identity with the simplicity and refinement of a strong independent design studio. UAE flag palette: fresh green #008A58, vivid red #EE3344, charcoal black #142820, white/transparent space. Flat vector-like solid forms, sharp clean contours with subtly rounded tips, no shading or gradients. Only 3 shapes, no fine lines or extra details. Recognisable at 24 pixels. Symbol occupies 80 percent of a square canvas. NO TEXT of any kind. Output on a GENUINELY TRANSPARENT ALPHA BACKGROUND, with empty transparent pixels outside the mark; do not draw a checkerboard or any background. This must look radically different from a heavy enclosed D-shaped document logo.
```

## Ask Docaya AI logo

Saved as [public/brand/ask-docaya-mark-v2.png](../public/brand/ask-docaya-mark-v2.png). New image, no reference image.

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a premium modern standalone SYMBOL for Ask Docaya AI, the companion assistant to a UAE document-intelligence brand with open flowing page ribbons. The symbol is an airy abstract conversation-orbit made from TWO elegant tapered curved page ribbons and ONE small red circular node. A broad emerald-green ribbon curves upward from lower left around an open white centre to upper right; a shorter charcoal-black curved page mirrors below it, leaving the orbit OPEN. A single vivid red circular node floats in the open gap at upper right. The flowing page ribbons should convey a helpful intelligence and knowledge in motion, with clean generous negative space and a distinctive asymmetric balanced silhouette. Fresh, optimistic, minimal, refined contemporary software identity. No enclosing badge or tile. No D monogram, no speech-bubble container, no sparkle or star, no robot face, no circuit pattern, no text. UAE flag palette: green #008A58, red #EE3344, charcoal #142820, white negative space. Flat vector-like solid forms, smooth clean edges, gently rounded tips, no gradients or shading. Exactly three separate substantial shapes readable at 24px. Centre the symbol in a square canvas with 10 percent safe margins. OUTPUT GENUINE TRANSPARENT ALPHA outside the mark, empty transparent pixels, no checkerboard drawn and no background.
```
